# 1. Percentage ka Basic Meaning

**Percentage ka matlab hota hai — Per 100**

Matlab:

**10% = 10 per 100**

Percentage ko fraction mein badalne ke liye:

> **100 se divide karo.**

### Example

10% = 10 ÷ 100 = 1/10

20% = 20 ÷ 100 = 1/5

25% = 25 ÷ 100 = 1/4

50% = 50 ÷ 100 = 1/2

37.5% = 37.5 ÷ 100 = 3/8

---

# 2. Fraction ko Percentage mein kaise badlein?

Fraction ko percentage mein badalne ke liye:

> **100 se multiply karo.**

### Example

1/2 × 100 = 50%

1/4 × 100 = 25%

1/5 × 100 = 20%

3/8 × 100 = 37.5%

# 4. Percentage ko Fraction mein Samajhna

Lecture ka important concept:

Agar:

**10% = 1/10**

to yahan **10** original number ko represent karta hai aur **1** uska percentage result.

### Example

10 ka 10%:

10 ka 10% = 1

Yahan:

- Original number = 10
- Percentage value = 1

Isi concept ko percentage ke questions mein use karna hai.

---

# 5. Kisi Number mein Percentage Add Karna

Agar kisi number mein usi number ka percentage add kiya gaya hai, to percentage ko pehle fraction mein badlo.

### Example

Kisi number mein 20% add karna hai.

20% = 1/5

Matlab agar original number ko **5 parts** maanenge, to 20% = **1 part**.

So:

Original = 5 parts

Add = 1 part

New number:

5 + 1 = 6 parts

Matlab 20% add karne ke baad:

**100% + 20% = 120%**

---

# 6. Percentage Ghataana

Agar kisi number se 20% kam kiya gaya:

100% − 20% = 80%

Matlab number ka **80% bachega**.

### Example

Number = 100

20% = 20

New number:

100 − 20 = 80
